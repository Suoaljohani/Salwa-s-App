# Release gates

## Blocking correctness
- Four output surfaces agree on metric, filters, and dataset version.
- Missing data is visible and never silently becomes zero.
- Obsolete responses cannot overwrite current filters.
- Imports do not double-count duplicate or overlapping sources.
- Private data and files cannot be read without the allowed account.
- No private key appears in frontend output.
- Filled Excel opens without repair and preserves the agreed template features.

## Product and craft
- The initial page is Assistant.
- Five tab positions match the product context.
- Arabic text, mixed numerals, units, and dates remain legible.
- Glass never obscures labels or data.
- Navigation works during dashboard motion.
- Repeated tab switching does not replay a long entrance.
- Reduced motion, transparency reduction, keyboard access, and text scaling work.
- Sound is opt-in and supplementary.

## Evidence to collect
Record browser, viewport, device/emulation status, data fixture version, and commands actually run. Use a small screenshot set: Chat, Indicators settled, Indicators during transition when recording is possible, import issues, and completed report preview. Do not store or distribute screenshots containing real patient data.

Fix blocking issues and relevant accessibility problems first. Do not turn the review into unrelated architecture refactoring. If a tool or real-device test is unavailable, report that limitation plainly.
